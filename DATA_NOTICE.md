# Dataset notice

The benchmark files in `Benchmark Dataset/` are processed versions of the
seven experimental single-cell RNA-seq datasets used by the BEELINE benchmark:
`hESC`, `hHEP`, `mDC`, `mESC`, `mHSC-E`, `mHSC-GM`, and `mHSC-L`. The fixed
edge splits in `data/` are provided solely to reproduce the evaluation protocol
used by DCR-GRN.

The upstream BEELINE data archive is available from Zenodo:

- https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.3378975

Please cite the original BEELINE publication when using these data:

> A. Pratapa, A. P. Jalihal, J. N. Law, A. Bharadwaj, and T. M. Murali,
> "Benchmarking algorithms for gene regulatory network inference from
> single-cell transcriptomic data," Nature Methods, vol. 17, pp. 147–154,
> 2020. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41592-019-0690-6
